What is the Dangote Refinery IPO?

The Dangote Petroleum Refinery & Petrochemicals complex, built in the Lekki Free Trade Zone in Lagos, is the world's largest single-train oil refinery — a project that's already reshaped Nigeria's fuel supply since it began operations in 2024.

The company, part of the Dangote Group, is preparing to go public through an Initial Public Offering (IPO) on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) — reported in the financial press as likely to be one of the largest public listings in African history, with a portion of the company's equity expected to be floated to raise capital for further expansion.

Reported estimates of the offer size and company valuation have varied as the process has developed, and — as of August 2026 — the exact offer price, subscription window, and final allocation terms have not yet been officially confirmed. Those details will be set out in the official prospectus once regulatory approval is granted by the SEC.

Figures below reflect public reporting as of August 2026 and are provided for general context only. Final terms of the offer will be confirmed in the official prospectus.

Everything about the offer, in plain language.

An Initial Public Offering (IPO) is the process through which a private company offers its shares to the public for the first time, allowing investors to become shareholders in the company.

The Dangote Refinery IPO is the proposed public offering of shares in Dangote Refinery. Once launched, eligible investors will have the opportunity to purchase shares in the company, subject to the terms of the offer.

The IPO will only be available once all necessary regulatory approvals have been obtained and the offer has been formally announced. BGL Securities will communicate the official opening and closing dates once they are confirmed.

Dangote Industries has indicated its intention to make the offer widely accessible to Nigerians, with further details to be provided in the official offer documents upon launch.

Once the offer opens, you can register to participate through BGL Securities using any of the following channels:
